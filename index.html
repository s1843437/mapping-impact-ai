Section 1 - Website setup assignment and project description

Original description

In this site I have to visualize the impact of AI through data. In order to do this I plan to rely on standard HTML for semantic structure and CSS for styling, responsive layout design, and managing visual variables. In the past I used C#, Javascript, PHP, and Perl to develop websites so I am planning to use one of them to elaborate data. Finally, Git and GitHub Pages will be used for version control and project deployment.

For data handling, I plan to use lightweight formats like JSON or CSV to store datasets on AI impact in the MFN domain. Raw data will be cleaned, structured, and transformed through standard database queries and dedicated scripts. Finally, the processed data will be rendered dynamically on the client side, ensuring a responsive, interactive, and accessible user experience.

First assignement

Mapping the Impact of AI: Adoption, Cybersecurity, and Digital Transformation in the Public Administration

Main Question

How is the adoption of Artificial Intelligence evolving within European and Italian Public Administrations, and what are the main challenges related to cybersecurity, internal technical skills, and regulatory compliance?

Supporting Questions
  1. What is the current level of AI adoption across European and Italian public sector entities, and in which service areas is it most frequently applied?
  2. What are the primary barriers to AI implementation in the Public Administration (e.g., lack of internal ICT skills, regulatory constraints, budget limitations, or data privacy and cybersecurity concerns)?
  3. How is the regulatory framework evolving (specifically with the European AI Act), and what governance requirements does it introduce for public sector organizations?

Your intended audience

This website is designed for public sector managers, Digital Transition Officers (RTD), Chief Information Security Officers (CISO), students, researchers, and citizens interested in an evidence-based understanding of real-world AI adoption in the Public Administration and its organizational and regulatory implications.

Data at hand

Links to promising datasets, with a brief description of what each contributes

  • European Commission Joint Research Centre (JRC) - AI Watch & Public Services

    Provides structured mappings, use-case inventories, and analytical reports on the adoption and impact of AI across European public services. Scientific AI Hub The home of JRC data

  • Istat - Permanent Census of Public Institutions & ICT Statistics

    Supplies national statistical indicators regarding digital maturity, ICT infrastructure, cybersecurity practices, and emerging technology adoption within Italian public administrations. Censimento permanente delle Istituzioni pubbliche: risultati preliminari anno 2022

  • Eurostat - Digital Economy and Society (e-Government indicators)

    Offers harmonized European comparative benchmarks on digital public services, e-Government interaction, and institutional digital readiness across EU Member States. Digital economy and society Database

  • European AI Act Service Desk

    Provides official documentation on risk-level classifications (particularly high-risk systems affecting public services) and compliance timelines for public institutions

  • United Nations on Artificial Intelligence

    Offers global public-sector governance frameworks and policy guidelines to contextualize international institutional standards.

Initial visualization ideas and any concerns about data availability.

Initial Visualization Ideas
  • Category Comparisons: Bar charts comparing AI application domains across public services and ranking the most reported barriers to adoption (cybersecurity, skills, regulation).
  • Spatial Maps: Choropleth maps displaying the geographic distribution of AI strategies and adoption rates across European Public Administrations.
  • Change Over Time & Timelines: A regulatory timeline illustrating key milestones of the European AI Act alongside trends in public-sector digitalization.
  • Infographic Summaries: Visual cards combining icons and key indicators to summarize risk classifications and organizational impacts.
Data Availability and Concerns

Unlike the private sector—where standardized enterprise surveys are updated annually—granular, uniform statistics on actual AI deployment within local and regional Public Administrations are often more fragmented or rely on periodic institutional censuses and pilot-project inventories. To address this limitation, our analysis will combine official quantitative indicators (from Istat and Eurostat) with structured qualitative mappings of European public-sector use cases curated by the JRC.
